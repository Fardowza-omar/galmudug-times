Careers at Galmudug Times

Join us in telling Somalia's most important stories

Why Work With Us?

Galmudug Times is a growing digital news organisation at the heart of Somali journalism. We are building a team of committed reporters, editors, and digital professionals who believe in the power of independent media to inform and empower communities.

Impact

Your work will reach and inform hundreds of thousands of readers across Somalia and the diaspora.

Independence

We maintain strict editorial independence - your journalism is guided by facts, not politics.

Growth

We invest in our team's professional development through training and mentorship.

Flexibility

Many of our roles offer remote or hybrid working arrangements.
