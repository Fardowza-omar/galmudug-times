What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and deliver a better experience. Some cookies are essential for the site to work; others are optional.

How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
