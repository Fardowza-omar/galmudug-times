Who We Are

Somalia's Independent Voice

Galmudug Times is an independent digital news organisation dedicated to delivering accurate, fair, and timely reporting on events across Somalia and the world. Founded with a commitment to quality journalism, we serve readers in the Galmudug region and throughout the Somali-speaking diaspora.

We believe an informed public is the foundation of a healthy democracy. Every article we publish is guided by the principles of accuracy, independence, and public interest.

Our Mission

Why We Exist

Our mission is to be the most trusted source of news and information for the people of Galmudug and Somalia — delivering stories that matter with honesty and integrity.

In a media landscape often defined by misinformation and bias, Galmudug Times stands apart. We are committed to raising the standard of journalism in Somalia — reporting facts, not narratives, and holding power accountable on behalf of the people we serve.

Our Principles

The Values That Guide Us

Accuracy

We verify every fact before publication and correct errors promptly and prominently.

Independence

Our editorial decisions are made free from political or commercial pressure.

Fairness

We present all sides of a story and treat every subject with respect and dignity.

Transparency

We are open about our methods, funding, and corrections policy.

Editorial Standards

Our Commitment to Quality

All content published on Galmudug Times adheres to our editorial standards. Our journalists follow a strict code of ethics based on the principles of the International Federation of Journalists. Stories are reviewed by senior editors before publication.

We welcome corrections and reader feedback at editors@galmudugtimes.com.
