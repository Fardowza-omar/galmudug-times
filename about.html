About Galmudug Times

Somalia's Trusted News network - Independent, Accurate, Accountable

Who We Are

Galmudug Times is an independent digital news organisation dedicated to delivering accurate, fair, and timely reporting on events across Somalia and the world. Founded with a commitment to quality journalism, we serve readers in the Galmudug region and throughout the Somali-speaking diaspora.

We believe an informed public is the foundation of a healthy democracy. Every article we publish is guided by the principles of accuracy, independence, and public interest.

"Our mission is to be the most trusted source of news and information for the people of Galmudug and Somalia - delivering stories that matter with honesty and integrity."

Our Values

  • Accuracy: We verify every fact before publication and correct errors promptly and prominently.
  • Independence: Our editorial decisions are made free from political or commercial pressure.
  • Fairness: We present all sides of a story and treat every subject with respect.
  • Accountability: We hold power to account - locally, nationally, and globally.
  • Transparency: We are open about our methods, funding, and corrections.

Our Coverage

Galmudug Times covers news across all major areas of public life: politics and governance, business and economics, technology, world affairs, culture, and opinion. We place a special focus on the Galmudug region, Muqdisho, and broader Somali affairs that affect everyday citizens.

Editorial Standards

All content published on Galmudug Times adheres to our editorial standards. Our journalists follow a strict code of ethics based on the principles of the International Federation of Journalists. Stories are reviewed by senior editors before publication. We welcome corrections and reader feedback at editors@galmudug-times.com.

Contact the Newsroom

Have a tip, correction, or story idea? Reach our editorial team at newsroom@galmudug-times.com or use our contact form.
