Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I submit a news tip?

Use the contact form above and select "News Tip" as the subject, or email us directly at newsroom@galmudugtimes.com. Tips can be submitted anonymously.

How do I report an error in an article?

Select "Correction Request" in the contact form and include the article title and the error you found. We take corrections seriously and will update the article promptly.

Does Galmudug Times accept advertising?

Yes. We offer digital advertising placements across our site. Contact our advertising team at ads@galmudugtimes.com for rates and availability.

How long does it take to receive a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during working hours (Saturday–Thursday, 8 AM–6 PM EAT).
